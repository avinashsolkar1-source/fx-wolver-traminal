LEARN

Trading basics, in plain words

Short lessons to read before you place your first trade. This is education, not financial advice.

What is a pip?
A pip is the standard small price step. For most pairs like EURUSD it is 0.0001, and for yen pairs like USDJPY it is 0.01. If EURUSD moves from 1.0850 to 1.0860, it moved 10 pips.
Pips measure distance. Your money result depends on how many lots you trade.
What are lots and position size?
A lot is the trade size. One standard lot is 100,000 units of the base currency. For EURUSD, one pip on 1 lot is about $10, on 0.10 lots about $1.
Stop 30 pips, 0.20 lots on EURUSD: about 30 × $2 = $60 at risk.
What are leverage and margin?
Leverage lets you control a bigger position than your cash. At 1:100, $1,000 of margin can hold about $100,000 of position. It makes gains and losses bigger, so it raises risk. Margin is the money set aside to keep a trade open.
What do Stop Loss and Take Profit do?
A Stop Loss closes the trade at a loss level you choose, so one bad trade cannot grow without limit. A Take Profit closes it at a gain level. In the FX WOLVER terminal you drag both on the chart and see the dollar risk and reward before you click.
How much should I risk per trade?
Many traders risk a small fixed share of the account, often 1% or less, so a losing streak does not end the account. There is no number that is right for everyone. Decide your risk first, then use the stop distance to find your lot size, which is what the calculator above does.

Questions

Do my traders have to come back to the website?

No. After the first login the terminal opens straight away. The website is only needed to sign up or read about the product.

Can a prop firm use its own rules and brand?

Yes. Your name, logo and colors load from a link, and the rules (profit target, daily loss, max loss, trading days) are set for your firm. Loss limits are checked on equity, so open trades count.

Which markets can I trade?

Forex, metals, indices, crypto and US stocks, with prices from live market feeds.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The terminal runs in any modern browser on laptop and phone, with a big BUY and SELL bar on mobile.

Why not just white-label MT4/MT5?

You can, and many firms do — it's the established route and worth comparing. FX WOLVER's difference is setup: no server license, no plugin for challenge rules, no separate CRM to wire up. Your branding and challenge rules load from one link, live in the browser, ready the same day.
