Risk disclosure

Trading foreign exchange, indices, commodities, shares and crypto assets carries a high level of risk and you can lose some or all of your money. Leverage can magnify both gains and losses. Do not trade with money you cannot afford to lose. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

The FX WOLVER terminal currently runs in a simulated environment with virtual funds. It does not hold, deposit or withdraw client money. Examples, animations and calculators on this site use sample numbers. Nothing here is investment advice.

Terms of use

  • The terminal is provided as is, for education and simulated trading.
  • FX WOLVER is not a broker, bank or regulated investment firm at this time.
  • Prop firm partners set and are responsible for their own challenge rules, fees and payouts.
  • Do not misuse, copy or attack the service.

Privacy

The site stores only what it needs: your settings in your own browser (local storage), and the details you send through the contact form, which opens in your email app. We do not sell personal data. Market data and fonts may load from third-party providers.
